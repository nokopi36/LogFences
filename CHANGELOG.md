# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Minecraft 26.3 / NeoForge 26.3.0.42-beta (Beta)

First release.

- Added log fences and log fence gates for 13 wood types: Oak, Spruce, Birch, Jungle, Acacia, Dark Oak, Mangrove, Cherry, Pale Oak, Poplar, Bamboo Block, Crimson Stem and Warped Stem.
- Added per-part bark stripping with an axe. Fences have 3 parts (post, upper rail, lower rail). Fence gates have 4 parts (end posts, inner posts, upper rails, lower rails).
- Added stripped variants, crafted from stripped logs and placed fully stripped.
- Recipes: 12 fences or 4 gates from logs; 6 fences or 2 gates from Blocks of Bamboo.
- Added the Log Fences and Gates creative tab. Items are also listed in the Building Blocks tab.
- English and Japanese translations.
